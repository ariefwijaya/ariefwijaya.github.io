Adaptations: short effects converted to mono 32 kHz / 96 kbps MP3 for browser compatibility; printer excerpt capped at four seconds with a short fade-in/out; wave converted to the same delivery format. Bird recording retained as delivered. Volume is mixed in Web Audio. A short crossfade is applied to the wave buffer before looping. No synthesizer or oscillator-generated sound remains.

Playback: audio remains off until opted in. Buffers are cached once, maximum eight concurrent voices, footsteps vary across three recordings, environmental sea playback is restricted to Islands, bird cue follows the visible bird cycle. Paper/Night intentionally have no unrelated ambient drone. Machine audio follows the four-second paper-conveyor action. Sound is suspended when the tab loses focus.

Night ambience: `night-city.mp3`, Joseph SARDIN, Paris by Night #0680, CC0, source https://bigsoundbank.com/paris-la-nuit-s0680.html (license verified 2026-09-14). A 40-second mono 24 kHz/64 kbps excerpt, high-pass 90 Hz/low-pass 2200 Hz to suggest distant traffic, fade-in/out plus runtime loop crossfade. Enabled only in Night when the user turns sound on. Replaces the previous absence of Night ambience.
