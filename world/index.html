How to explore

  • Walk: W A S D, arrow keys, or the movement joystick. A gentle joystick push walks slowly; a full push walks faster.
  • Look around: choose First person or Third person, then drag the scene or use I J K L. On touch screens, use the left joystick to walk and the right Look pad to turn at the same time. You can also drag the scene.
  • Camera: open Explore → Camera view, or press C to cycle views. Diorama and Top view keep a fixed viewing direction.
  • Choose a destination: click or tap the floor in Diorama or Top view.
  • Open a story: select an object or its marker. Hover or focus a marker to see its name. A nearby action appears when you walk closer; press E or tap it.
  • Take the short route: open Explore (M), then choose Places to explore or Guided tour.
  • Take a break: select the lounge chair. Moving stands you up.
  • Change the mood: switch the lamp or enable sound.

Calm motion reduces camera movement and decorative animation. Your device’s reduced-motion preference is respected automatically.
