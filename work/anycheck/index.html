The product

AnyCheck brings document verification and extraction into a shared workspace. Its capabilities include custom extraction templates, bank statement analysis, an AI assistant and a visual Workflow Builder.

The workflow tooling lets users configure a process from the node types available to their organisation. Upload and results views connect that process to the documents being handled.

My contribution

At Fintelite, I lead technical direction and help connect architecture and engineering delivery with Product and Data team priorities.

My document-AI experience includes OCR pipelines, LLM integration and work with the Data Team on extraction improvements. I work alongside the engineers and product teams responsible for the wider platform.

From document to next action

  • Review a document beside its structured fields and line items.
  • Ask Orbit about the document without leaving its context.
  • Connect extraction and follow-up steps in the visual Workflow Builder.
  • Monitor the organisation’s activity, credits and review queue.
  • Configure custom extraction fields and refine their definitions.
  • Inspect financial-document account totals and transaction-level records.
