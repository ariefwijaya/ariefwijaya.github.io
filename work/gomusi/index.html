The product

GoMUSI organises public-service information and applications around forms and a sequence of service steps. Its interfaces include service selection, submissions, review and submission details.

Administrative tools support the management of services, forms and ordered flow or wizard steps.

My contribution

I worked on the frontend and backend. The scope includes applicant-facing interfaces and the application services behind forms and service flows.

The administrative side is part of the product scope: teams need to manage what applicants see and the stages a service requires, rather than relying only on a fixed public form.

Selected product flows

  • A catalogue of services and their requirements.
  • Multi-step application and information collection.
  • Submission details, progress and review.
  • Administrative form, service and flow management.

For a similar project

This work is relevant to a business or institution replacing disconnected forms with a structured application process.

That experience also applies to application portals, intake workflows and administrative tools that need to work together.
